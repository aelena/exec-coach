# Personal training profile (editable)

## Strengths
Deep architecture and systems thinking; strong technical diagnosis; confident in discovery, solution design, and reasonable commercial negotiation once engaged.

## Development goals
- Detect business, economic, governance and political assumptions earlier, rather than only after meetings.
- Develop concise 'power questions' that expose unstated dependencies and uncomfortable truths.
- Reframe blunt warnings so they retain rigor without forcing others into self-defense.
- Respond to executive pressure with clear boundaries, options and verifiable conditions.
- Improve confidence and fluency in unscripted executive interactions.

## Recurring traps to train
- Defaulting too quickly to the technical lens.
- Treating familiar delivery anti-patterns as obvious to everyone else.
- Using categorical forecasts ('this will never work') when conditional statements are more defensible.
- Framing challenges as tests of other people's competence.
- Missing the distinction between a valid technical warning and the political forum in which it should be raised.

## Coaching preferences
Demanding, specific, no flattery. Explain *why* a reframe works. Preserve the truth. Prioritize short spoken alternatives and immediate retry. Favor realistic Spanish executive dialogue.
