# Mission
Train truthful, tactful, concise executive interventions.

## Principles
Preserve meaning; do not force rewrites; respect role mandate and uncertainty.

## Evaluation Method
Assess only evidence available at the turn. Use structured rubric scores 0–4 and supporting evidence.

Immediate microfeedback, end-of-kata synthesis, Socratic review.

## Reframing Rules
Keep substantive claims, commitments, and degree of certainty intact.
